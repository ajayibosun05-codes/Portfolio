01 · About

I bridge engineering, code and people

I'm studying metallurgical engineering at the University of Lagos while building real software on the side. I manage projects, write front-end code, and I understand the physical side of making things — which gives me a different perspective from most developers.

Metallurgy is about precision and process. You can't rush a heat treatment and expect good results — the same goes for software. That's shaped how I approach every project: define the scope clearly, break it into pieces, and deliver each piece properly.

I'm from a background where resources are tight, so I learned early how to prioritize and get results without waste. I volunteer with a community at my school because I want the people around me to have better opportunities — and I want the technology I build to do the same.

02 · Work

NutriScanBioDrive hackathon, University of Lagos · 16 July 2026

The problem

Hypertension and chronic kidney disease are both heavily diet-related, but food labels don't tell you anything useful if you don't know what to look for.

What I did

I ran the project and built the interface in a two-person team — handled the scope, broke the work into daily tasks in Notion and Jira, and coded the front end.

Outcome

A barcode-scanning prototype that checks packaged food against a user's health profile using a rule-based risk score. We didn't win, but we shipped a working product in two weeks, which was the real goal.

Built with: React, Open Food Facts API, barcode scanning. Managed with: Notion, Jira.

SolidWorks CAD ModelsEngineering coursework

The problem

Orthographic engineering drawings need to become accurate 3D parts that hold up under manufacturing review.

What I did

Converted 2D drawings into 3D models, including a P-clip pipe strap bracket. Used the sheet metal Base Flange method to keep features clean and editable.

Outcome

A reliable modeling workflow I still use. CAD is one of the skills I'm most confident in, and I've got fast at diagnosing sketch and extrude errors that used to slow me down.
